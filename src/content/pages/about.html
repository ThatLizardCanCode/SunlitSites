What We Do

Sunlit Sites Mission & Vision

We're Hannah and Jayden, a couple based on the West Coast who help small businesses get online without the stress or massive price tags.

Hannah handles all the design and development hand-coding every website from scratch (no templates, no shortcuts). She's the tech brain who makes sure your site loads fast, looks great, and actually works.

Jayden manages everything else the admin, client communication, photography, and making sure projects stay on track. With a background in business and accounting, he keeps things running smoothly behind the scenes.
